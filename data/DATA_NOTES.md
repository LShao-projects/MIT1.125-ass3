# Dashboard data provenance

The reproducible country seed is generated by `python3 scripts/prepare-data.py` from `../research/eu-coverage-snapshot-2026-10-03.json`, audited on 2026-10-03. The source coverage and exclusions are explained in `../EU_DATA_COVERAGE.zh-CN.md`. `seed.json` is a compact presentation extract; the full source rows and Eurostat response stay in the research snapshot.

## Measures and limits

- `price` is Eurostat nrg_pc_205, 2025-S2, IG consumption band (at least 150,000 MWh/year), EUR/kWh, excluding VAT and recoverable taxes (`X_VAT`). The Eurostat response was updated 2026-09-24T23:00:00+0200. This is a national average for large non-household consumers, not a data-center tariff or a site quote.
- A positive price is `available` for 16 countries; Austria's positive flagged estimate is `estimated`. Four confidential and two missing values have `price: null`. Cyprus, Estonia, Latvia, and Lithuania returned zero and are `review` with `price: null`, pending an explanation. Thus 17 prices can support a qualified reference calculation, and 10 cannot.
- Generation, demand, renewable share, generation mix and total-generation carbon intensity are taken from Ember's 2024 country rows. `generationTwh` uses the **Total generation** row; `demandTwh` uses the **Demand** row. `renewableShare` and `mix` are percentages of generation. `carbonIntensity` is gCO2e/kWh for total generation. These values are not guaranteed to be directly measured for every country.
- `dcRecords` and `clusterRecords` count country matches in the downloaded Epoch AI data-center and GPU-cluster files. Zero means absent from those files, not zero facilities. Cluster phases may overlap, and the two data sets are not a national census. The six `cases` are concrete Epoch records for exploration, not validated site offers. In particular, EcoDataCenter 2 has zero current reported power and must not be treated as operating capacity.
- `priority: true` identifies the initial Germany, France and Sweden deeper-study examples, not an investment ranking. National data alone do not establish available grid connections, site water, fiber, equipment costs or demand commitments.
- Every source is `verificationStatus: pending`. The German, French and Swedish official pages were reviewed as candidates during the source audit, but no named team reviewer/date has completed formal verification. RTE and Ember emissions figures use different boundaries and must not be interchanged.

## Map

`public/europe.geo.json` is derived from [Natural Earth 1:50m Admin 0 countries](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-cultural-vectors/50m-admin-0-countries-2/) via its [GeoJSON distribution](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson), downloaded 2026-10-03. Natural Earth states its vector data are [public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). The file retains the 27 EU members, clips to longitude -12..37 and latitude 34..72, and simplifies at 0.02 degrees. It uses Eurostat `EL` for Greece and corrects Natural Earth's special France ISO code to `FR` for joining; `ISO_A2`, `code` and `ISO_A3` are in the feature properties. This is a generalized visual map, not a legal boundary source or survey map. The European crop omits overseas territories and distant islands.

## Refresh rule

Re-run the script only against a newly audited frozen snapshot after reviewing source dates and price flags. A new download must not inherit any future human verification status from the previous version.

## Context map (2026-10-03)
`public/world.geo.json` derives from Natural Earth's 1:50m admin-0 countries, simplified with Shapely at 0.035 degrees while preserving topology. Antarctica is omitted from this infrastructure comparison map. Mercator latitude is clipped at ±80 degrees. Boundaries provide geographic context, not evidence of data coverage or political status. The EU dataset drives membership colours; selection and price coverage are separate map layers. Country names appear consistently on hover, with project metrics only for EU dataset members. Europe and world extents share the same geometry and preserve aspect ratio.

## Retrieval audit — 2026-10-05

The [retrieval manifest](source-retrieval-audit.json) records the actual UTC access time and SHA-256 hash of each newly downloaded source. The complete Ember and Epoch CSVs were downloaded again. Every field of all 459 archived Ember EU 2024 rows, three archived Epoch datacenter rows, and 54 archived Epoch GPU-cluster rows matched the newly retrieved files. This establishes a real retrieval time for the unchanged records used by the dashboard; it does not backdate the original 2026-10-03 download. The German, French, and Swedish official pages were also downloaded successfully, which establishes link access time but **not** human verification of their claims.

Eurostat price records and `S-EUROSTAT` receive a retrieval timestamp only after a successful, validated API refresh. The source URL now specifies the same `2025-S2` observation used by the refresh parser. `S-CALC` is the internal deterministic calculator, so an external retrieval timestamp does not apply. All external source records currently have a documented access time. Human verification remains pending separately.

On restricted local hosts, Miniflare's outbound fetch can fail even when the host Node process can reach Eurostat. Development mode therefore exposes a fixed-target, loopback-only Vite bridge for this one public Eurostat endpoint. Production Workers fetch Eurostat directly. The app's existing validation, atomic D1 update, role checks, and failure retention still run through `/api/refresh` in both environments. This local success does not replace a hosted production refresh test.
